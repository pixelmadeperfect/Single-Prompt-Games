THE COACH’S CLIPBOARD

How to Herd the Team

Touch Controls

  • Move: drag the joystick left or right.
  • Pounce: press POUNCE while moving—the controls support two fingers.
  • Smash: press POUNCE again while airborne and close to a high ball.
  • Block: press POUNCE at the net as an opponent’s shot arrives.
  • Switch: tap a teammate or the ↻ CAT button.

Desktop Controls

  • Move: hold A/D or ←/→. Mouse dragging also works.
  • Pounce / Smash: Space, W, ↑, or left-click the court.
  • Switch: Q, Tab, click a teammate, or use ↻ CAT.
  • Pause: Esc or the pause button.
READ THE COURT The landing paw predicts the ball. Clean hits, blocks, and accidental setups build MEOW SPIKE; mistakes drain it. At 100%, ULTRA locks in until your next smash. Stronger rivals may throw suspicious food—watch the warning banner.
